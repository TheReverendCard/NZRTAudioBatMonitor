## Overnight comparison

Host verification used the two original COM14 desk WAVs, each 768,044 bytes.  Each went through the receiver as a manual event and two identical audio uploads.  Each reconstructed file matched the original SHA-256 byte for byte.  Separate tests cover a late LoRa anchor, unknown UTC, wrong format, bad checksum, rejected credentials and conflicting duplicate identities.  The receiver configuration and archive root resolved on the existing PC.  These checks do not exercise the field board's SD socket, RF link or timer wake.  The firmware stores cumulative Wi-Fi enabled milliseconds and worst verified SD commit time per boot in health.csv for the next USB-meter trial.

Use the union of **human-reviewed passes** from the co-located recorders during overlapping, time-aligned coverage.  Inspect unmatched detections in both directions and account for microphone placement, frequency coverage and clock uncertainty.  Report pass recall with its denominator, false alerts per listening hour, fraction with verified SD WAV, fraction received, and median/95th-percentile alert delay.  Compare first PC receipt with trigger UTC only for adequately synchronised clocks.  Start with a USB-meter reading before dusk and after sunrise; take a second reading at the next dusk for a full 24-hour budget.  Divide usable battery Wh by measured Wh/day, accounting for the intended regulator.  The USB-powered board cannot establish cell runtime directly.

A short power cut may leave a .part or .tmp.  Acknowledge only complete verified WAVs.  Preserve remnants for inspection, retain original SD WAVs after successful uploads, and keep the permanent reference firmware available as the rollback.  Timestamp estimates are tied to the audio task's frame-read time.  DMA, oscillator and LoRa beacon timing uncertainty still need a common acoustic pulse measurement for sub-second cross-monitor matching.


## Pulse-train candidate, 25 November 2026

A grade-5 XiaoRT calibration clip recorded at 02:54:16 NZDT on 18 November had a maximum score of 1,404.  Jesse saw five well-defined clicks among six.  The clip-level maximum used to calibrate the threshold discards the repetition visible in FastAviaReview.  The preceding grade-5 clip at 02:54:14 scored 2,877.  Treat their relationship as a question for pass-level review.

The repeated-pulse trial reuses the pinned frame model, adds a three-onset decision, and retains the strong-frame path.  The initial score floor of 702 (half the existing cutoff), release of eight below-floor frames and first-to-third window of 187 frame intervals are engineering trial settings.  They are not fitted species-specific pulse intervals.  Default shadow mode annotates existing clips.  An SD marker opts into additional sequence-triggered capture and reporting.  The README describes each mode, archive and receiver metadata, and local replay.  The audio task adds three small integer counters for the pulse detector and a comparison per score without another FFT or model evaluation.  Additional recordings and transfers in record mode can increase whole-device energy use.

## Event-driven power trial, 25 November 2026

The experimental unit was running Stream 02 during the meter comparison.  Same-place USB input energy was 0.18 Wh in 31:06 without a receiver IP and 0.22 Wh in 31:30 with it reinstated, approximately 0.347 W and 0.419 W.  The paired difference was approximately 0.072 W.  A single pair cannot separate boot, RF, candidate traffic, SD and listening costs.  The 11 clips from boot 4280DF29 contained one confirmed bat.  Keep thresholds fixed for the power comparison and check missed passes against independent recorders.

Stream 04 builds on the pulse-train trial.  It removes continuous Wi-Fi association in legacy UDP mode and timer-only Wi-Fi refresh.  Authenticated replay examines the card before requesting Wi-Fi.  WAV backup and RAM rescue both require explicit opt-in.  A verified SD WAV remains the primary audio copy.  Unacknowledged UDP messages have a bounded volatile queue.  HTTP has persistent event identity, timestamps and acknowledgements.  Bulk WAV retries run in the daylight maintenance window or an explicitly enabled manual transfer.  Receiver and archive remain compatible with Field 03 metadata.

The packed FFT reduces complex butterflies from 2,304 to 1,024, then reconstructs 192 positive bins.  The band features, weights, strong threshold, pulse-state rules and input waveform are unchanged.  Floating-point ordering may differ, so the reference mode and per-frame replay test are retained.  A PDM density diagnostic samples one frame in 128.  CPU time is sampled for both the decoder and scorer, and the solar schedule is recalculated at most once per second in steady operation.  Check these measurements and Wi-Fi-on time on the card before drawing conclusions about battery life.

True acoustic-triggered deep sleep needs an always-on ultrasonic wake circuit or processor.  On the current board the Knowles PDM mic needs a host clock, and ESP32-S3 light sleep can stop or change the I2S source clock.  Skipping acquisition would create blind intervals and lose pre-trigger audio.  The stock board keeps DMA listening and blocks the capture task between reads.  Hardware references: [Espressif I2S power management](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/i2s.html#power-management) and [Knowles SPH0641LU4H-1](https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/930/SPH0641LU4H-1.PDF).
